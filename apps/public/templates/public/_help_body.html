Help

Asking about a flight

Once {{ pwa.name }} is added to Claude (below), ask about a flight by its number, such as "Where is BA117?" or "When does EZY8461 land?". Claude answers in words and with a map card: the airport it left, the way it has flown, where it is now, the way still to go and when it's due. Long-haul flights are drawn on a globe.

{{ pwa.name }} uses live data from Flightradar24, which knows a flight by the number of the airline flying it. If a codeshare number isn't found, ask with the operating airline's number: it's on your booking or the departures board. Flightradar24 has no airline schedules, so {{ pwa.name }} shows when a flight is due, not whether it's late.

Looking up a flight saves nothing about you.

Take-off and landing alerts

Follow a flight and {{ pwa.name }} tells you when it takes off and when it lands. Sign in, open the app and type the flight number under Follow a flight, or ask Claude to "follow BA117". {{ pwa.name }} follows the next flight with that number to take off, or the one in the air now.

Alerts come by push notification, by email, or both: turn either off for each flight on the Following list. To get push notifications, tap Turn on and allow notifications. On an iPhone or iPad, add {{ pwa.name }} to your Home Screen first and turn them on from there: Safari only sends notifications to apps on the Home Screen. Every alert email has a link that stops alerts for that flight.

An alert usually arrives within a few minutes of Flightradar24 seeing the take-off or landing. A flight that doesn't take off within a day is dropped, and a landed flight leaves the list the next day.

Signing in

There are no passwords. Enter your email address on the sign-in page and we'll send you a one-time six-digit code, with a link that signs you in with it. Use either within 15 minutes. The first time you sign in, this creates your account.

If you've installed the app on an iPhone or iPad, type the code into the app rather than opening the link: the link signs in Safari, not the app.

Passkeys

Once you're signed in, open Account and choose Add a passkey. From then on you can sign in with Face ID, a fingerprint or your device PIN, using Sign in with a passkey. You can remove a passkey from the same page.

Installing the app

On iPhone or iPad, open the app in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android or a desktop browser, use Install in the browser's menu.

Using it offline

Pages you've opened while signed in work without a connection. Looking up a flight needs one, as the data is live.

Use it from Claude or ChatGPT

{{ pwa.name }} is a connector: you add it once, and your AI chat uses it when you ask. You need this address:

{{ mcp_url }}

It is for your AI chat to use, not a page to open in your browser.

Claude

  1. On claude.ai or in the Claude desktop app, open Customize, then Connectors.
  2. Choose +, then Add custom connector.
  3. Give it the name {{ pwa.name }}, paste the address into the URL field, leave Advanced settings empty and choose Add.
  4. Choose Connect. Claude opens the {{ pwa.name }} sign-in page: enter your email address, then the six-digit code sent to it.
  5. Start a new chat and ask. If Claude doesn't use {{ pwa.name }}, open the + menu under the message box, choose Connectors and turn {{ pwa.name }} on.

ChatGPT

ChatGPT adds connectors through developer mode, on the web only. Business, Enterprise and Edu plans can use every tool; Pro can use only the ones that read. In a workspace, an admin turns developer mode on, or allows you to.

  1. In ChatGPT on the web, open Settings, then Apps, then Advanced settings, and turn on Developer mode.
  2. Back in Apps, choose Create. Give it the name {{ pwa.name }}, paste the address and choose OAuth.
  3. Choose Scan Tools. ChatGPT opens the {{ pwa.name }} sign-in page: enter your email address, then the six-digit code sent to it. Then choose Create.
  4. {{ pwa.name }} is now under Settings, Apps, marked Dev. Start a new chat and ask.

Your AI chat sees what your account holds, and nothing of anyone else's. To disconnect it, remove the connector in your AI chat's settings.
